The Artist

About

Charly Ponziani grew up between Italy and Northern Ireland, and later lived in France, England, Spain, and Belfast. It was in Belfast, away from where she started, that she began to write her own music.

She is a pianist, vocalist, and performer, creating intimate, cinematic work. Alongside composing, she sings and performs, with a particular focus on the wedding industry, where music plays a deeply personal role. Her vocal style is rooted in jazz, soul, and blues, and she also enjoys exploring other genres, including electronic music, where she has collaborated as a vocalist.

Music was always part of her life, but it was in Belfast that it became her own. Her composition Aurelia was featured on BBC Introducing. She works in both Italian and English, with Spanish as an additional language. She is now based in Milan.
