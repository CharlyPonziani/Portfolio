The Artist

About

Charly Ponziani is based in Milan. She was born and raised in Umbria, Italy, where she completed her early education. The daughter of an Italian father and an Irish mother, she grew up between the two countries — between languages, cultures, and ways of living. She later lived in England and Spain before settling in Belfast, where she began writing her own music.

She is a pianist, composer, vocalist, and performer, creating intimate, cinematic work across both media composition and live performance. Alongside composing for visual and narrative projects, she also sings and performs, with a particular focus on the wedding industry, where music holds a deeply personal meaning.
Her vocal style is rooted in jazz, soul, and blues, while remaining open to a wider range of influences, including electronic music, with which she has also collaborated as a vocalist.

Music had always been part of her world, shaped by a musical family —
but it was in Belfast that she found her own voice within it.
Her original piano composition Aurelia was recently featured on BBC Introducing.
Separately, she composed an original score for a short film, which was later performed and recorded by the Ulster Orchestra in Belfast.
She graduated in 2026 with a Foundation Degree in Music and Sound for Media (a two-year programme in performance and production, validated by The Open University, UK).
She works in both Italian and English, with Spanish as an additional language.
